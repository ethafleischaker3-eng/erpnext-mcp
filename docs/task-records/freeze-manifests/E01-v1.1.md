1. `MCP Business Caller` 角色 DocPerm 由 21 个 DocType 增至 **23 个**：追加 **Account read+select**、**Cost Center read+select**（`permlevel=0`、`read=1`、`select=1`；`write/create/submit/cancel/delete/amend/report/import/export/share/print/email/set_user_permissions` 一律 `0`）。
